# Data

`raw/opportunities_snapshot.csv` is a curated, metadata-only snapshot.

It contains role title, company, opportunity type, location, work mode, skills,
dates, source URL, eligibility notes, and observation date.

It intentionally does not contain applicant information, CVs, recruiter contact
lists, or copied full job descriptions.

## Refresh policy

Career pages change frequently. Before applying:

1. Open the original source.
2. Confirm the role is still available.
3. Confirm location and work authorization.
4. Confirm the deadline.
5. Tailor your CV honestly.
